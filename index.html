Privacy Policy for TallyWorth

Last updated: September 10, 2026

TallyWorth is a personal finance app built by David Huseonica / DJH Systems & Integration. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

The short version

TallyWorth has no backend server. There is no DJH Systems & Integration database, no analytics, and no advertising. Your financial data lives on your own devices and syncs privately through your own iCloud account. We never see it, store it, or have access to it.

What data the app handles

TallyWorth stores the account, balance, holding, and transaction information you enter or import: account names, balances, dates, and related notes you choose to add. This data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework and, if you have iCloud enabled, synced across your own devices via Apple's CloudKit — using your personal iCloud account, subject to Apple's own privacy policy.

AI-powered statement import (optional)

TallyWorth can optionally use AI to read PDF bank or brokerage statements and extract balances, holdings, and transactions. This feature is opt-in and works as follows:

  • Apple Intelligence: processing happens entirely on your device. Nothing leaves your device for this option.
  • Claude, Gemini, or ChatGPT: if you choose one of these, the text of the statement you're importing is sent directly from your device to that provider's API, using an API key that you obtain and enter yourself. That request goes straight from your device to the provider — it never passes through any server we operate, because we don't operate one. Use of these providers is governed by their own privacy policies (Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI respectively).

API keys

API keys you enter for AI providers are stored only in your device's Keychain, protected by Apple's system-level encryption. We never transmit, collect, or have access to your API keys.

Backup files

If you use the app's export feature, TallyWorth creates a JSON backup file containing your account data (never your API keys). This file is created and stored wherever you choose to save it — we have no access to it.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect analytics or usage data.
  • We don't show ads or work with ad networks.
  • We don't sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.
  • We don't have a server that your financial data passes through.

Children's privacy

TallyWorth is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
